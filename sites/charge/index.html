    Shared chargers at As Oy Koivurinne

    Residents of As Oy Koivurinne (a simulated housing company) send one night-slot request before 18:00. Charging runs 22:15–07:00, at most 11 kW per car, and the housing company plans every car fairly under its 22 kW limit and signs a receipt. Ask the assistant to send yours: you see the details and confirm first, this site's server signs it, and a request can't be changed once sent.

    No resident code saved. It stays on this page and is sent only to the housing company through this site's server; the assistant never sees it. No code? Get a simulated one-night demo resident code on the housing company's page.

      How it works

      • Finnish electricity is priced per 15 minutes. Tomorrow's prices are published around 14:00.
      • We choose the cheapest periods between now and your ready-by time, at your charger's power.
      • This page only plans. It does not control your charger or buy electricity.
      • Sharing chargers with neighbours? As Oy Koivurinne, a simulated housing company, shows how shared chargers can be planned fairly. This page's assistant can ask the housing company's assistant too, after you agree.
