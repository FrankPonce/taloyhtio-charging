Four shared chargers, one fair plan.

The residents of As Oy Koivurinne share four car chargers. At night they share one power limit, so requests are planned fairly: earliest deadline first, cheapest slots, never over the limit.

As Oy Koivurinne is fictional. It is part of the Trust Before Transaction demo; no real residents or bills are involved.

The rules

  • 4 chargers, 11 kW each, in the shared parking area.
  • 22:00–07:00: the chargers share 22 kW.
  • Daytime: first come, first served.
  • You pay for your own slots only.
